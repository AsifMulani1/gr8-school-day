Parent questions

The questions parents ask first.

Does AI replace a teacher or mentor?

No. Human mentors provide targeted subject teaching, individual guidance and academic challenge. AI helps personalise practice and gives mentors insight into understanding.

How much time will my child spend on screens?

Screen-based learning is mainly in the morning, alongside teaching from human mentors. Afternoons are hands-on: labs, industry interaction, arts, sport and reflection.

Will my child still study the core subjects?

Yes. A protected three-hour Academic Core every morning covers English, Mathematics, Science, Humanities and a second language: French, Hindi or Spanish.

Is GR8 a separate campus?

No. GR8 runs as a school within a school at GEMS Modern Academy, Kochi, with shared friendships, sport, arts and activities.

Is there homework?

Yes, but it looks different: applied projects plus short AI-supported catch-up where the child needs it.

When does admission open, and what will it cost?

Admissions launch in January 2027 for Grades 6, 7 and 8; the first year begins in June 2027. Fees will be shared in INR when admissions open.
